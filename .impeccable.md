## Design Context

### Product
Malta Invoice Flow Pro — a web-based invoicing tool for Maltese small businesses. Handles invoice creation, client management, VAT (18%), and PDF export with banking details.

### Target Audience
Mixed audience: freelancers/sole traders, small business owners (1–10 staff), and accountants/bookkeepers managing invoices on behalf of clients. Skill range spans non-finance people who need clarity to experienced bookkeepers who want efficiency.

### Use Cases
1. Create and send professional invoices quickly
2. Apply correct Maltese VAT rates and generate VAT summaries
3. Customise invoice templates to match company branding
4. Manage client records and payment tracking
5. Export PDFs for email / record-keeping

### Brand Personality
**Clean & efficient · Modern & polished · Friendly & approachable**

The interface should feel like a tool that respects the user's time — minimal friction, obvious actions, no unnecessary complexity. Warm enough that a non-finance sole trader doesn't feel intimidated, but precise enough that a bookkeeper trusts the numbers.

### Aesthetic Direction
Inspired by **Stripe / Linear** — confident use of dark accents and tight spacing, clean information hierarchy, precision in every detail. NOT flashy or decorative. Complexity is earned, not imposed.

### Theme
Light mode primary. Users work during business hours in mixed environments (offices, home desks, phones). Dark mode is secondary.

### Color
Slate Navy (Option B) — `hsl(222, 55%, 30%)` primary. Professional without being cold. Tinted neutrals toward the navy hue.

### Row Density
Comfortably compact — users with many invoices/customers need more rows visible. Base `TableCell` uses `py-2.5 px-4` (10px vertical / 16px horizontal). Rows sit at ~40px, not 52px.

### Anti-references
- Avoid generic SaaS dashboard look (cyan accents, gradient cards, hero metrics with glowing borders)
- Avoid anything that looks like a generic Tailwind/shadcn starter template
- Avoid cluttered sidebar navigation with nested accordions everywhere
